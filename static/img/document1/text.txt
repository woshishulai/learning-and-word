英语短文背诵文件
一、英文原文
A teenager hid behind loose, dusty curtains, packing his overcoat into a suitcase.

He planned to leave home at dusk, even though it was thundering and lightning outside.

He had to do this because he was fed up with his parents' nagging about his English learning and couldn't bear it any longer.

He wasn't good at English and didn't like attending English classes because he felt the teacher ignored him.

His partner was very worried about him. She fully understood what he was going through but completely disagreed with his idea.

To calm him down and solve his problem, she talked with him face to face and shared a series of tips on learning English with him.

The learning methods she wrote down helped him find the "path" to learning English well.

The teenager was very grateful and gained great strength from his friend's words.

Now, he has recovered from his depression and fallen in love with English.

二、中文翻译
一个少年躲在松松垮垮、积满灰尘的窗帘后面，把他的大衣收拾进行李箱。

他计划在黄昏时离开家，尽管外面雷电交加。

他不得不这样做，因为他已经厌烦了父母对他英语学习的唠叨，也不想再继续忍受下去。

他英语学得并不好，也不喜欢参加英语课，因为他觉得老师忽视了他。

他的搭档非常担心他。她完全明白他正在承受什么，但完全不同意他的想法。

为了让他冷静下来并解决他的问题，她和他面对面交谈，并和他交流了一系列学习英语的技巧。

她记下的那些学习方法帮助他找到了学好英语的“道路”。

这个少年非常感激，并从朋友的话中获得了很大的力量。

现在，他已经从沮丧中恢复过来，并且爱上了英语。
